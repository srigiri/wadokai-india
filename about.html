OUR PHILOSOPHY

Karate builds the person, not just the fighter.

Wadokai Karate-Do brings together technique, movement, discipline and character. Training is designed to help students become stronger, more confident and more responsible.

Our dojo culture places respect at the centre: respect for instructors, training partners, parents, opponents and the responsibility that comes with strength.
